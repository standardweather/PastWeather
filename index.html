PastWeather

Add an end date, to total several days

Start here: enter a place, a UTC date and a time. You get the radar at that moment, the day's hail and rotation swaths, storm reports, warnings, lightning, nearby observations, and a written account to paste into a report.

Other ways to use it:

  • Leave the date blank to see the place's hail, wind and tornado history back to 1950.
  • Add an end date to total wind and rain over up to 31 days.
  • Date of loss compares a claimed date with the days around it. Ice & snow shows freezing weather before the time.

Radar and swaths are available from October 14, 2020.

Data sources · not a certified weather analysis

Radar, hail, rotation and rain: NOAA MRMS. Reports: NOAA SPC (preliminary) and NCEI Storm Events (official). Observations: NCEI and the NWS. Warnings: NWS, archived by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Lightning: NOAA GOES GLM. Normals and records: NOAA ACIS. Not a substitute for a certified weather analysis.

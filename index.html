PastWeather

Enter a place and a UTC date and time. PastWeather shows NOAA radar at that moment, the day's hail-size and rotation swaths, storm reports, and measured observations from the nearest stations. Radar and swaths are available from October 14, 2020.

Map layers

Radar, hail and rotation: NOAA MRMS. Reports: NOAA SPC (preliminary) and NCEI Storm Events (official). Observations: NCEI and the NWS. Not a substitute for a certified weather analysis.
